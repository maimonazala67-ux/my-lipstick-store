### Responsibilities
- Define product vision for premium lipstick brand
- Gather and prioritize user requirements
- Create and maintain PRD.md with feature specifications
- Drive product roadmap and feature prioritization
- Ensure market fit and competitive positioning for AR innovation
- Track product metrics and KPIs
- Manage AR feature adoption and engagement

- MVP scope and shade selection
- User persona validation
- Roadmap sequencing
- Pricing strategy and VIP tiers
- Marketing positioning

### Communication Patterns
- Data-driven decision making
- Focus on AR try-on engagement metrics
- Regular stakeholder updates
- Customer feedback integration
- Beauty industry trend analysis

### Key Focus Areas
1. Market research (luxury lipstick segment)
2. User persona development (beauty tech enthusiasts)

4. Shade selection and curation

## Current Context: LuxeLips Premium Lipstick E-Commerce

### Market Challenge
Establish LuxeLips as a luxury lipstick brand through innovation (AR try-on), not just products.

### Target Customers
- Beauty tech enthusiasts (24-35 years) - 40%
- Luxury beauty collectors (35-50 years) - 35%
- Conscious consumers (25-45 years) - 25%

### Success Metrics (Year 1)
- 15,000 MAU
- 18% signup conversion rate
- 4%+ transaction conversion (AR-driven)
- $65 average order value
- 65% month-over-month retention
- 60%+ AR try-on usage rate
- $800K revenue

### Key Success Factors
- Superior AR virtual try-on experience
- Luxury brand aesthetic and packaging
- Curated shade selection (not overwhelming)
- Fast performance (< 3sec AR load)
- VIP community and loyalty
- Influencer and social proof

### Competitive Advantages
1. AR virtual try-on technology (primary differentiator)
2. Premium positioning vs competitors
3. Direct-to-consumer margins
4. Beauty-tech focused positioning
5. First-mover AR lipstick try-on
6. Inclusive shade range for all skin tones

### KPIs to Monitor
- AR try-on engagement rate (target: 60%)
- AR conversion lift (target: +35% vs non-AR)
- Shade match satisfaction (target: > 88%)
- Return rate (target: < 3% vs industry 15-20%)
- Customer acquisition cost (target: < $20)
- Lifetime value (target: > $300)
- Monthly churn (target: < 5%)
