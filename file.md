# Graph Engineering File: Website Development for Delicious Tomato Shop

## PHASE 0 — DISCOVERY & RESEARCH

### Business Problem
- Client runs a tomato shop named "Delicious Tomato Shop."
- Objective is to create a website branding the tomato shop and enable online perfume sales.
- Need to unify diverse product offerings under cohesive branding, with a secure and scalable e-commerce platform.

### Target Users
- Local customers familiar with the physical shop.
- Online buyers seeking perfumes.
- Users looking for trusted and branded local products.

### Industry Standards
- Secure e-commerce platforms (PCI Compliance for payments).
- Responsive and accessible web design.
- Clear navigation and user-friendly product browsing.
- SEO and digital marketing best practices.

### Competitors
- Local perfume shops with online sales.
- Regional grocery or specialty shops extending into perfumes.
- Established perfume e-commerce websites.

### Similar Successful Products
- Websites blending specialty food retail with fragrance product lines.
- Well-branded local store websites with product catalogs and online shopping.

### Market Expectations
- Fast, intuitive online shopping experience.
- Mobile and desktop friendly.
- Brand story that connects tomato shop reputation with perfume quality.

### Technical Feasibility
- Integration with commonly used payment gateways.
- Inventory management combining tomato and perfume products.
- Scalable backend architecture to allow future product expansion.
- Secure user authentication and data protection.

### Identify
- What to Build: E-commerce website focusing on perfume sales with strong tomato shop branding.
- What Not to Build: Complex marketplace or unrelated product categories initially.
- Risks: Brand dilution mixing two categories; security in payment processing; SEO confusion.
- Missing Requirements: Detailed product catalog, inventory sync needs, marketing plan.
- Alternatives: Launch perfume store first with tomato branding, expand product range later.
